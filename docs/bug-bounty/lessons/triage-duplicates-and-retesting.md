# Handle triage, retesting and disclosure professionally

## Outcome and prerequisites

Continue the reporting workflow without overstating impact or extending authorization. Complete the reproducible-report lesson. Program and platform terminology differ. No valid finding, acceptance or reward is required to complete the course.

## Program-based workflow

1. Submit through the designated platform route only after confirming asset, method and disclosure rules. Use minimal reproducible evidence and redacted controlled-account data.
2. Read triage feedback fully. Identify whether clarification, evidence or a retest is requested. Do not answer uncertainty with more intrusive testing.
3. If duplicate, record the lesson without demanding details of another researcher's confidential report. A duplicate outcome does not itself establish a mistake in your testing.
4. Retest only under current permission and within the original agreed issue. Reuse the minimum controlled proof, with a positive control where safe. Do not turn a retest into an expanded assessment.
5. Distinguish fixed, not reproduced, blocked by changed prerequisites and untested. A failed reproduction can have several causes.
6. Apply the current disclosure policy before sharing any detail. A report being resolved, informational or out of scope is not automatic permission to publish.

Bugcrowd publishes separate disclosure and retest guidance. Read the current policy and program brief rather than using a remembered waiting period or assuming that every platform uses the same states. When ambiguous, keep the material private and ask through the authorized route.

## Optional local private log

In a new scratch directory, create synthetic notes only:

```bash
cat > report-log.csv <<'CSV'
date,program,state,next_action
YYYY-MM-DD,synthetic-program,drafted,review-before-submission
CSV
cat report-log.csv
```

Expected: the header and one synthetic row. For real reports, retain platform IDs and private notes only in approved storage. Do not put passwords, bearer tokens or private program details in a public repository.

## Questions and answers

A triager requests more impact. May you access unrelated users' data? No. Explain the limitation, use controlled accounts where authorized or request a coordinated plan.

A report is resolved. May you publish? Not without satisfying the applicable disclosure policy and required approvals.

Is a payout guaranteed for a technically correct report? No. Eligibility, scope, duplication and program policy affect outcomes. Keep earnings expectations separate from learning progress.

## Troubleshooting and cleanup

Use documented platform support or mediation routes for disagreements, not off-platform pressure. Remove credentials and excess evidence according to policy; preserve only what is permitted for the record.
