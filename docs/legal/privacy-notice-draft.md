Final notice wording must match the actual deployed fields and logging.

Do not place passwords or unrelated sensitive information in enrollment fields. Reading confirmation and eligibility are self-declared, not independently verified.

## Approved retention schedule

| Record category | Operator-approved policy |
| --- | --- |
| Approved enrollment/access records | Retain until designated-admin-confirmed course completion. Completion ends that course's access. Delete a shared record only after all active courses are complete. |
| Denied, withdrawn, or abandoned applications | Delete at the end of the third following Monday-Friday date after explicit admin closure, using America/New_York. Exclude the closure date/weekends; do not exclude holidays. No automatic inactivity timer is approved. |
| Administrative audit events | Delete three calendar months after each event timestamp in UTC; preserve time of day and clamp to the destination month's last valid date where necessary. |

Audit events have their own schedule and may remain after enrollment/access deletion. These are policy targets, not a claim of implemented deletion or exact execution timing. The current pure deadline calculations do not select/delete records or schedule jobs. The retired signed-document retention period is not used. No new schedule for unrelated lab, incident, legacy progress, or provider data is established here.
