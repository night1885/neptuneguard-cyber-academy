## 1. Education and separate approvals

The Academy provides cybersecurity education and controlled training. Enrollment approval, authorization for a particular lab, and certificate awards are separate decisions. A permanent academy student ID identifies an approved enrollment; it is not a professional license, certificate, or permission to test systems. This agreement and general course material do not themselves authorize testing.

## 2. Eligibility and limited information

Learners must be 18 or older and personally answer Yes to the age-18-or-older declaration. This is self-declared eligibility, not independently verified age or identity. Enrollment does not collect actual age in years, birth dates, legal names, government identification, guardian documents, signed documents, or video. A minor-enrollment or guardian-consent workflow is not provided by this policy.

Do not submit those items, addresses, email addresses, passwords, or unrelated sensitive information through enrollment fields or public channels. The separate privacy notice draft describes the limited registration/access records and outstanding operational verification. Neither this draft nor that notice is approved for production publication.

## 3. Account acknowledgment and registration

Registration may begin with an admin-created record or a learner application. The learner must personally read the identified approved agreement version, explicitly acknowledge it through their own Discord account, confirm reading, complete the approved comprehension check, make the eligibility declaration, and submit for a separate admin decision. An admin-created registration does not supply declarations on the learner's behalf. Reading and eligibility are self-reported; a comprehension result is not independent proof of reading, identity, or age.

No typed-name signature or signed upload is required or accepted by this workflow. Registration and submission do not grant access or issue a permanent ID. Approval precedes initial ID issuance. Assigned IDs are bound to the corresponding Discord account; sharing an ID does not authorize another account. Pending, denied, suspended, or revoked enrollment does not grant course access. The staged section-entry flow checks the assigned ID and current authorization. Deployment and live acceptance testing are required before describing the restoration as operational.

## 4. Authorized scope and prohibited activity

Before testing, obtain explicit authorization from a party with authority over the targets. It must identify targets, permitted activities/tools, exclusions, start and expiration times with timezone, additional rules, approver, emergency contact, and stop conditions. Missing, unclear, expired, suspended, or revoked authorization means no testing. Accessibility, discovered vulnerabilities, or possession of credentials do not expand scope. Academy approval cannot authorize third-party systems outside its authority.

Unless expressly authorized by applicable lab rules, do not conduct disruptive or destructive testing, denial of service, phishing, social engineering, physical intrusion, unauthorized malware deployment/persistence, credential misuse, collection or disclosure of personal data, movement into unapproved systems, or evasion of monitoring/access controls. Public services, Academy production infrastructure, Discord systems, other learners, and third-party devices/accounts are excluded unless separately authorized by an appropriate party. General lessons do not override lab restrictions.

## 5. Stop-work, confidentiality, and integrity

Stop immediately when instructed, when authorization expires or is withdrawn, when scope is uncertain, when disruption occurs, or when unexpected third-party targets or sensitive data are encountered. Report privately to eadred@neptuneguard.com. Do not continue beyond scope or publish sensitive findings while awaiting instructions. The operator must verify the incident contact and process before activation.

Use lab information only for authorized learning/reporting. Do not post passwords, tokens, personal information, or sensitive findings publicly. Avoid unnecessary copying and follow approved storage, reporting, cleanup, and deletion instructions. Confidentiality does not prevent legally protected reporting or compliance with lawful process.

Do not impersonate others or falsify declarations, work, or access records. Distinguish actual execution, self-reported completion, simulated output, and outside assistance. Do not claim credentials that have not been issued.

## 6. Enrollment and access records

The proposed workflow retains necessary Discord-linked records: Discord user ID, application/approval status, eligibility and acknowledgment status, agreement version/hash, comprehension-check version/result, relevant timestamps, approval/audit records, and permanent academy ID if issued. Permission for these records does not authorize unrelated progress, quiz-answer history, XP, badge, activity, or provider tracking.

Limit record access to authorized personnel with an appropriate administrative, security, or legal need. Final privacy wording must identify the responsible operator, actual purposes, providers, access/disclosures, request handling, and verified limitations. Do not claim zero retention, universal erasure, or unverified security controls. See privacy-notice-draft.md.

## 7. Approved retention policy and completion rules

These periods are operator-approved policy requirements; automated enforcement is not yet verified:

- Approved enrollment/access records are retained until course completion. The designated academy admin confirms completion; viewing the last lesson does not automatically count. Completing one course ends its access. Delete a shared enrollment/access record only after all of the student's active courses are complete.
- Denied, withdrawn, or abandoned applications are deleted three business days after explicit admin closure. No automatic inactivity timer is approved. Use America/New_York, exclude the closure date, and count the next three Monday-Friday dates. Weekends are excluded; holidays are not. The deadline is the end of the third counted date.
- Administrative audit events are deleted three calendar months after each event occurs, calculated in UTC with its time of day preserved. If the corresponding date does not exist, use the destination month's last valid date. This is not a fixed 90-day period.

Audit history follows its separate schedule and may remain after enrollment/access deletion. The retired five-year signed-document schedule does not apply. No new schedule for unrelated lab, incident, legacy progress, or provider records is established by these decisions.

Before activation, implement and verify completion/closure signals, shared-record handling, related-record dependencies, deletion scheduling/retries, restricted permissions, and the distinction between eligibility deadlines and actual execution. Pure deadline calculators are not deletion jobs. Review applicable preservation requirements and proposed exceptions separately. Provider logs, backups, and recoverable copies require verification; do not promise immediate or universal erasure. Existing historical records are not erased by this draft.

## 8. Access changes and agreement versions

The Academy may deny, suspend, or revoke access for incomplete records, suspected misuse, violations, or safety concerns. Stop affected activities even if a technical session remains open. Reinstatement requires explicit approval and does not automatically restore lab authorization. Direct access-review requests to eadred@neptuneguard.com.

Acknowledgment applies to the exact identified agreement version. Do not silently substitute terms or overwrite a published version. Present material changes for renewed acknowledgment before access requiring updated terms is granted. Confirm that learners can read and obtain a copy of the final approved agreement and privacy notice before activation.

## 9. Materials, fees, and complaints

Use Academy materials for authorized learning. Redistribution, publication, or commercial reuse requires permission unless permitted by applicable license or law. Learners retain rights in their original work subject to third-party rights. Any permission for the Academy to retain/use submissions requires a defined purpose and separate review; no unrestricted publicity license is granted here.

This draft establishes no pricing, payment/refund policy, accreditation, or certificate-issuance process. If fees are charged, disclose approved prices, cancellation/refund terms, assessment criteria, and credential requirements before commitment. Participation does not guarantee employment, licensing, third-party certification, or exam results.

Comply with applicable law; nothing authorizes unlawful conduct or waives rights that cannot lawfully be waived. No mandatory arbitration, exclusive venue, liability cap, indemnity, or broad waiver is established here. Direct questions/complaints to eadred@neptuneguard.com without restricting legally available remedies. Proposed lawful-disclosure and incident procedures require review before publication; requests alone do not authorize unrestricted disclosure.
