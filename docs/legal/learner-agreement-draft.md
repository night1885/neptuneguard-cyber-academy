# NeptuneGuard Cyber Academy — Learner Agreement Draft

Status: DRAFT — not approved for learner acknowledgment, publication, or enrollment activation.
Version: DRAFT-2.
Operator: neptuneguard.
Operator location: Massachusetts, United States.
Academy contact: eadred@neptuneguard.com.
Effective date: Not set.

This document records proposed terms and user-specified policies. It does not certify legal compliance or represent that consent, record-copy, retention, deletion, or security controls are implemented. Obtain Massachusetts legal review before use.

This revision implements the acknowledgment-only direction in acknowledgment-only-policy.md and supersedes DRAFT-1's signed-upload requirements. Neither draft is approved for publication. No signed agreement upload or document-storage service is part of this workflow.

## 1. Purpose and separate approvals
The Academy provides cybersecurity education and controlled training. Enrollment, lab authorization, and certificate awards are separate decisions. A permanent student ID identifies an enrollment record, not a professional license or earned certificate. This agreement does not authorize testing of any system.

## 2. Required enrollment records
The learner must read the approved agreement version, type their full name to acknowledge it, explicitly confirm reading, pass the approved comprehension check, and submit an enrollment request for explicit owner approval. No signed-document upload is required or accepted by this enrollment workflow. Submission alone grants no student ID, course access, lab authorization, or certificate. Falsified acknowledgment or impersonation is prohibited. Reading confirmation is self-reported; page navigation does not independently prove reading.

## 3. Electronic acknowledgment
Before submission, the learner must be shown the approved agreement, asked to affirm their intent to acknowledge it electronically, and given an approved way to obtain a copy. Records are associated with the authenticated Discord account, exact agreement version and content hash, acknowledgment timestamps, and comprehension-check version. A typed name is not conclusive proof of identity. Final consent, attribution, accessibility, and record-copy processes require legal and implementation review before activation.

Proposed learner declaration, subject to that review: I have read NeptuneGuard Learner Agreement version [VERSION]. I agree to its training rules, understand the stop-work requirements, and understand that enrollment approval does not authorize lab activity or award a certificate. I intend my typed full name to record my acknowledgment.

The current typed-name and READ fields must not be represented as proof that every proposed consent and copy-delivery requirement is implemented. Review the actual displayed form and submitted records against the final approved declaration before activation.

## 4. Lab-specific authorization
Before testing, each lab requires explicit authorization from a party with authority over its targets. Authorization must identify targets, permitted activities and tools, exclusions, start and expiration times with timezone, additional rules, approver, emergency contact, and stop conditions. Missing, unclear, expired, suspended, or revoked authorization means no testing. Accessibility, discovered vulnerabilities, or possession of credentials do not expand scope. Academy approval cannot authorize third-party systems outside its authority.

## 5. Prohibited activities
Unless expressly authorized in the applicable lab rules, learners must not conduct disruptive or destructive testing, denial of service, phishing, social engineering, physical intrusion, unauthorized malware deployment or persistence, credential misuse, collection or disclosure of personal data, movement into unapproved systems, or evasion of monitoring and access controls. Public services, Academy production infrastructure, Discord systems, other learners, and third-party devices or accounts are excluded unless separately authorized by an appropriate party. Accounts and credentials must not be shared for impersonation. General lessons do not override specific lab restrictions.

## 6. Stop-work and incidents
Stop immediately when instructed, when authorization expires or is withdrawn, when an unexpected third-party target or sensitive data is encountered, when disruption occurs, or when scope is uncertain. Report privately to eadred@neptuneguard.com. Do not continue beyond scope or publish sensitive findings while awaiting instructions. The operator must verify that this contact supports its approved incident-response process before activation.

## 7. Confidentiality and learning integrity
Use lab information only for authorized learning and reporting. Do not post passwords, tokens, sensitive findings, or personal information in public channels. Follow approved storage, reporting, cleanup, and deletion instructions. Avoid unnecessary copying of unexpected sensitive information. Submit work honestly and distinguish actual execution, self-reported completion, simulated output, and outside assistance. Do not claim credentials that have not been issued. Confidentiality does not prevent legally protected reporting or compliance with lawful process.

## 8. Access changes
The Academy may deny, suspend, or revoke access for incomplete records, suspected misuse, violations, or safety concerns. Learners must stop affected activities even if a technical session remains open. Reinstatement requires explicit approval and does not automatically restore lab authorization. Review requests may be sent to eadred@neptuneguard.com.

An issued permanent student ID is preserved when access is suspended or revoked; the ID is not itself current authorization. Database authorization changes do not terminate running provider sessions. The operator must separately review and implement any required provider-side session termination.

## 9. Privacy and authorized access
The proposed enrollment workflow records Discord user ID, typed full name, agreement version and content hash, acknowledgment and learner-confirmed-read timestamps, comprehension result and check version, approval and access audit records, and permanent student ID if issued. This is personal enrollment metadata, not merely an approval-status flag. No signed agreement document is uploaded or retained by this workflow.

Collect only information reasonably needed for enrollment, training, security, administration, or legal obligations. Do not request government identification numbers, financial details, passwords, or unrelated sensitive information in enrollment fields. Limit access to authorized neptuneguard personnel with a relevant need, and document material disclosures. Before collection, approve and publish a privacy notice covering data, purposes, actual service providers, access, retention, disclosures, and available request procedures. Actual database hosting, access, encryption, backup, and deletion controls require review; this draft makes no claim that enrollment metadata is stored only on a local computer.

## 10. Retention, deletion, and legal preservation
Enrollment-metadata retention periods, retention start events, closure handling, deletion procedures, and backup-expiry treatment remain undecided. They must be approved before enrollment opens. The retired five-year and six-month signed-document storage proposals do not establish a metadata-retention schedule. Do not infer zero retention from the absence of uploaded documents.

Deletion is subject to applicable retention duties, valid court orders, and legal holds requiring preservation. Preserved records remain access-restricted and will be reviewed for deletion when the requirement ends. Secure disposal procedures must address active storage, recoverable copies, and backups. Do not promise immediate deletion from all backups unless supported by the actual process. Separate schedules are required for application metadata, audit events, course progress, lab records, and incidents. No existing records are deleted by this draft.

## 11. Court orders and investigation disclosures
Relevant records may be disclosed when required by a valid court order or other legally binding process, or when reasonably necessary and legally permitted to assist investigations into suspected unlawful activity, security incidents, or Academy misuse. An authorized reviewer will assess legal basis, relevance, scope, and recipient. Limit disclosure to what is necessary or required and document it. Notify learners where required or appropriate and permitted; do not promise notice when legally restricted. This clause does not authorize unrestricted access or disclosure solely because someone requests investigative assistance.

## 12. Information security and breach response
The operator will maintain an appropriate security and incident-response program and assess applicable Massachusetts and federal requirements. Suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or misuse will be evaluated. Legally required notices will be provided to applicable individuals, authorities, and other recipients according to the relevant requirements. Contract confidentiality does not prevent mandatory reporting. These are proposed obligations, not representations of current implementation.

## 13. Eligibility and minors
Minimum age and any guardian requirements must be decided and disclosed before enrollment opens. Do not approve applications involving minors until the operator establishes an appropriate eligibility, consent, privacy, and contracting process. Do not assume that a guardian acknowledgment alone resolves children's privacy requirements. No age rule is selected by this draft, and no age or guardian-verification control is certified as implemented.

## 14. Intellectual property
Academy materials may be used for authorized learning. Redistribution, publication, and commercial reuse require permission unless permitted by applicable license or law. Learners retain rights in their original submissions, subject to third-party rights, and grant the operator a limited permission to use submissions for assessment, administration, security review, and recordkeeping. No unrestricted publicity license is granted.

## 15. Fees, services, and certificates
If fees are charged, disclose pricing, cancellation, refund terms, assessment criteria, and certificate requirements before purchase or commitment. Enrollment and completion do not guarantee employment, licensing, accreditation, third-party certification, or exam results. Describe Academy credentials accurately. No payment or refund policy has been selected in this draft.

## 16. Applicable law and complaints
The operator will comply with applicable federal, Massachusetts, and other laws that apply to its activities and learners. Nothing authorizes unlawful conduct or waives a right that cannot lawfully be waived. If a provision is unenforceable, remaining provisions apply to the extent permitted by law. This draft does not establish exclusive court venue or mandatory arbitration. Complaints and record or access questions may be sent to eadred@neptuneguard.com without restricting legally available remedies. Liability caps, indemnity, arbitration, venue, and broad waivers require separate counsel review.

## 17. Versions and learner declaration
Acknowledgment applies only to the version identified in the enrollment records. Do not silently substitute terms. Present material changes for renewed acknowledgment before access requiring updated terms is granted. Approved text must have a final version identifier and content hash distinct from this unapproved draft.

The proposed declaration in section 3 confirms association with the authenticated Discord account, reading of the identified version, understanding of separate lab authorization and stop requirements, accuracy of the submission, and intent to acknowledge through a typed name. It requires no signed upload. Acknowledgment does not itself grant access.

Proposed record fields: authenticated Discord user ID; typed full name; agreement version and content hash; acknowledgment and learner-confirmed-read timestamps; comprehension-check version and passing result. Owner approval and permanent student ID, if issued, are recorded separately. Learners must not supply their own approval status, authoritative timestamps, or passing result.
