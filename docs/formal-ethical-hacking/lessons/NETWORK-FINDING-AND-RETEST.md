## 3. Claim classification
For each statement classify it as an observed fact, justified interpretation, hypothesis or unsupported claim. Explain the evidence needed to strengthen it:
- The endpoint accepted a TCP connection during the first check.
- A displayed service name proves the exact software version.
- A stopped teaching listener means every vulnerability was fixed.
- Two matching observations prove the entire host is secure.
- A timeout proves the firewall is the cause.

These are reasoning prompts, not results from an executed assessment. Do not alter scope to gather missing evidence.
