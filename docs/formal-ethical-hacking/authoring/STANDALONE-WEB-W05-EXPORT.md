## Exact fictional policy

Contributors may read their own active draft/submitted records and records shared for reading in assigned boards. Read sharing does not grant export. Contributors export only their own active records in assigned boards. Board-assigned reviewers read/export submitted records, not drafts or retired records. Unknown roles receive no records; malformed/missing assumed Actor context is rejected. Actor checks do not authenticate identity.

Returned detail/export records expose only id, title and state. Even an allowed row must not expose owner, board, sharing information or reviewer_memo. Every memo here is invented text, not a real secret.

## Investigation tasks

1. Build a subject/operation/record/property matrix. Trace detail and export separately; do not assume a correct detail path secures collection export.
2. Preserve two hypotheses before consulting the repair: which records enter the export, and which properties each returned row contains. Give a forbidden case, nearest legitimate control and competing explanation for each.
3. Trace the starter factory's selection of can_read and dict. Decide whether read permission is the correct export rule and whether copying all fields is the permitted projection. These are separate questions.
4. On paper, predict an operation-only repair and a projection-only repair. Which defect should remain in each? Preserve permitted shared detail reading and legitimate owner/reviewer exports rather than rejecting everything.
5. After separately approved execution, report actual cases/statuses and returned fields, not an author's transcript. Preserve original expectations and source identity. Do not broaden inputs, create a listener or supply real records.
