## Review criteria

Assess correct separation of policy, status, content, browser behavior and server facts; bounded impact; explicit unknowns; useful remediation/retest goals; and scope/privacy discipline. Require the learner's reasoning, not copied key phrases. Equivalent accessible responses are acceptable; record substantive hints and unattempted parts honestly.

Fresh protected variants and independent assessor calibration are required before formal grading. Proposed variant dimensions include ownership, lifecycle state, course assignment, status/content combinations and explicitly missing facts. Keep intended policy consistent and difficulty comparable; do not label a relabeled public example as validated unfamiliar-task independence.
