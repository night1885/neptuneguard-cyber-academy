## Case discussion keys

A: missing owner relationship permits a forbidden decision under the fixture policy. Role/course membership is insufficient. Review owner, nonowner, course, action and state cases; authoritative real-world identity/resource facts are not implemented by the toy model.

B: deny-all breaks legitimate owner-draft read/edit and assigned-instructor read behavior. Require positive as well as negative controls. An instructor editing and a student acting on a submitted record remain denied under this specific fixture policy.

C: bound values address query construction and preserve literal punctuation. They do not decide whether the caller may select another owner's record. Distinguish syntactic failure, returned row and business authorization.

D: value placeholders are not arbitrary identifier substitutions. Prefer fixed structure; if selection is needed, choose among reviewed fixed identifiers through trusted code. This discussion introduces no new query task.

E: preserve only the intended paragraph structure and original text. Stripping characters loses legitimate data. Context-appropriate encoding differs from sanitizing an allowed markup subset. Verify structure and decoded text, not only an encoded substring.

F: paragraph results do not establish safe JavaScript/URL handling. Require context-specific analysis and controls. Do not turn the paper question into script execution.

G: passing examples support tested model behavior only. No authentication, HTTP enforcement, database isolation, browser execution or full-app coverage follows automatically.
